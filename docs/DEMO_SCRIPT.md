# Demo video script (target 2:30)

**Before recording**
- Do one live run of your hero product the day before, so you know the timings, and keep `?replay=1` open in a second tab as the backup.
- Set `GEMINI_API_KEY` so chat understands free-form requests and the quality check runs.
- Use Chrome or Edge (voice input). Close other tabs. Zoom the browser to 110% so text is readable on video.
- Long waits (generation ~2 min, files ~20 s) are cut in editing. Say "(cut)" in your notes where you trim.

| Time | Screen | Say (roughly) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Start screen | "Every online shop wants its products in 3D for product pages, AR and printing. Today that takes a 3D artist and days of work. AI can now make a model in two minutes, but a raw model isn't a usable product asset." |
| 0:15–0:30 | Type or paste the brief, add the photo | "Aholo Creator Copilot is a production assistant. I describe what I need in plain language and add a photo." |
| 0:30–0:45 | Click **Create production plan**, point at the steps and the finish picker | "It plans the production for me: generate, quality-check, apply a finish, split into parts, export. I can review and change it before anything runs." |
| 0:45–1:10 | **Approve & run**, show the progress ring and timer, then cut to the model appearing | "It runs every step on Aholo Lux3D: image-to-3D with G1-Turbo, Material Transfer, Part Split and Multi-Format Export. The AI checks the result against my brief, and would regenerate if it fell short." |
| 1:10–1:30 | Rotate the model, click **Explode components**, then **Original** vs **Processed** | "Here's the finished model, with its parts separated by Lux3D Part Split, and the original before the finish." |
| 1:30–2:00 | Voice: press the mic and say a change in another language (e.g. Spanish: *"Hazlo de cobre cepillado"*); show the reply, or the credit confirmation for an expensive change | "I can change it by talking, in any language. It re-runs only the affected steps, and it asks before spending a lot of credits." |
| 2:00–2:15 | Ask: *"What files do I get for AR?"* | "It also answers questions about the project." |
| 2:15–2:30 | Export centre: download USDZ / GLB / OBJ | "And I get files ready for my store, Apple AR and 3D tools. From a sentence and a photo to an export-ready 3D product: that's Aholo Creator Copilot." |

**If live is slow on the day:** switch to the `?replay=1` tab. It plays a real recorded Lux3D run and is labelled "Demo replay", so say so: "this is a recorded run of the same pipeline, to save time."
